Weight in sets

Same weight across all sets (straight sets) — choose a weight you can hit the lower rep range with in every set.

Example: Bench press 4×5–7 at 100 kg — all 4 sets at 100 kg.

Pyramid sets have a flaw — early sets too light, last set too heavy. Only 1–2 effective sets instead of 4.

When to increase weight

  • 1Complete the upper rep range in all sets (e.g. 4×7 with range 5–7) → add 2.5 kg next session.
  • 2After increasing, drop back to lower range (e.g. 4×5) and build back up. This is double progression.
  • 3For isolations and finishers, add 1.25 kg or one rep — slower progression is normal.
  • 4Can't hit the lower range for 2 weeks in a row — reduce by 5–10% and rebuild.

Warm-up

Before the first compound of each session — 2–3 warm-up sets, never to failure:

Set 1: 40% × 10 · Set 2: 60% × 6 · Set 3: 80% × 3

Goal: warm up joints, prime the nervous system, enter working sets fully ready.

Deload — every 6–8 weeks

Deload week: 50–60% of normal weight · 2–3 sets instead of 4 · same rep range but with 2–3 reps in reserve · cardio and abs unchanged.

Supercompensation fully happens during deload. Personal records often follow.
Session length: Push and Pull ~70 min · Legs ~75 min · + 25 min cardio · total ~95–100 min. Use a timer for rest periods.

💪 Muscle Gain (Lean Bulk)
Add +200–250 kcal above your TDEE. Aim for 0.5–1 kg gain per month — more than that means excess fat.
🔥 Fat Loss (Cut)
Subtract 300–400 kcal from your TDEE. Keep protein high to protect muscle. Aim for 0.5–0.75 kg loss per week.
Key rules:
· Protein: always 2.0–2.5 g per kg of bodyweight — this is non-negotiable
· Carbs: your main energy source, adjust based on training intensity
· Fat: minimum 0.8 g/kg for hormonal health, don't go below
· Weigh yourself weekly (same day, same time) — 4-week average matters more than daily fluctuation
💊 Supplementation Guide
General reference info, not medical advice. Supplements support a solid diet and training plan — they don't replace either. Check with a doctor before starting anything new, especially if you take medication or have a health condition.
🧪 Creatine Monohydrate3–5 g / day
Increases phosphocreatine stores in your muscles, helping regenerate ATP faster during short, intense efforts. The single most well-researched supplement for strength and power — no loading phase or cycling needed, take it any time of day.
🥛 Whey Protein20–40 g / serving
A fast, convenient protein source for when whole food isn't practical. Use it to help close the gap to your daily protein target above — timing around your workout matters far less than your total for the day.
☕ Caffeine3–6 mg/kg bodyweight
Stimulates the central nervous system, improving alertness, focus and perceived effort — one of the most reliable performance enhancers available. Take 30–60 minutes before training; avoid within 6–8 hours of bedtime.
☀️ Vitamin D31,000–4,000 IU / day
Supports bone health, immune function and muscle performance. Widely deficient, especially with limited sun exposure or darker skin tone. Take with a meal containing fat for better absorption — a blood test can confirm your actual need.
🐟 Omega-3 (Fish Oil)1–3 g EPA+DHA / day
Supports joint health and reduces exercise-induced inflammation, alongside heart and brain benefits. Take with food to improve absorption and reduce the chance of an upset stomach.
🧂 Magnesium200–400 mg / day
Involved in muscle contraction, recovery and sleep quality — commonly under-consumed even with a solid diet. Best taken in the evening; glycinate or citrate forms are gentler on digestion than oxide.
⚡ Beta-Alanine3–6 g / day
Buffers acid buildup in your muscles, helping extend the number of quality reps in the 8–15 range. Causes a harmless tingling sensation (paresthesia) — split into smaller doses through the day if that bothers you.
🩸 Citrulline Malate6–8 g pre-workout
Improves blood flow to working muscles, which can reduce fatigue and let you push more volume across a session. Take 30–60 minutes before training.

📏 Body Measurements
How to measure correctly:
· Waist — at navel level, exhale naturally, don't suck in
· Chest — across nipple line, arms relaxed at sides
· Bicep — flexed, at widest point of dominant arm
· Shoulders — fullest part, arms at sides, tape parallel to floor
· Hips/Glutes — widest part of hips/buttocks
· Thigh — upper thigh, just below the glute fold, dominant leg
Measure in the morning, before eating, same conditions each time.

ℹ️ How to Use This App
💪 Training Tabs
Push, Pull and Legs are your three main training days. Whole Body is a 4th, optional full-body session. Each exercise shows LT (Last Training weight), SB (Season Best) and PB (Personal Best) — tap LT to log a new weight, tap SB or PB to reset them. A small since date next to SB and PB shows how long that record has stood. The ↑ Increase button flags an exercise so you remember to add weight next session; it clears automatically once you log a heavier LT.

The round ⏱️ button starts a rest timer — it keeps running even if you switch tabs or leave the app, and beeps at 30 seconds left and at zero. While it's running, a small roman numeral appears next to Increase (II, III, IV…) counting which set you're about to do — it moves with you if you start a timer on a different exercise, and clears once you finish the session.

At the bottom of the last exercise, 💪 Done logs the whole session and shows a summary: an estimated volume range (from the exercises you actually timed), any new SB/PB records, and anything flagged with Increase.
✏️ Editing Exercises
Swipe left/right on an exercise (or use the ‹ › arrows) to see alternative exercises for that slot. Edit changes an exercise's name, notes, sets, reps, rest time and type — and lets you delete it. ＋ Add Alternative adds a new option to that slot.

The Abs section at the end of every session is shared across all four days: edit it or log a weight once, and it updates everywhere.

Whole Body works differently — every exercise there is a link to one that already lives in Push, Pull or Legs, so weights, records and tips stay in sync automatically. Adding an alternative there opens a picker of your existing exercises instead of a blank form. Removing one from Whole Body only unlinks it; the original stays untouched.
💡 Tips
Tap 💡 Tips on any exercise for a full-screen view with LT/SB/PB and the rest timer. Every exercise comes with detailed, pre-written form guidance — training parameters and technique cues — which you can freely edit or replace with your own notes (bold, underline, bullet lists), plus an optional YouTube link for form reference.

Two mini charts show your SB and PB progress over the last 12 months. Tap either one to enlarge it, then swipe or use the arrows to page back through earlier periods.
📅 Calendar
Tap any day to log a completed session or plan a future one. Planned days show a dashed outline; once the date arrives you can mark it Done or cancel it. Mark a session as a Deload week when logging it — deload days get a small 🔻. Swipe left or right across the calendar grid to move between months. On the entry list below, swipe an entry left to delete or right to edit.

🥗 No Cheat Meal has two checkboxes: one for a clean day where you also trained, and one for a clean day with no training at all — checking the second grays out the training type and deload options and lets you log the day without picking a session type. Either way, a small 🥗 marks the day on the calendar and in the list.
📏 Body Stats
Log waist, chest, arms, shoulders, hips, thigh, weight and body fat %. Add up to 3 progress photos (front/side/back) from your camera or photo library — they're automatically compressed so your data stays small. Tap a photo to view it full-screen.

Log every field plus all 3 photos in one entry, and the app quietly starts a 24-day countdown — the next time you open the app on or after day 24, you'll get a push notification nudging you to measure again. Logging another full entry restarts the countdown.
📈 Charts
Eight views of your training and body data:
Body Measurement Trends — a line chart per measurement.
Weight & Body Fat — both plotted together on a dual axis.
Progress Photos — compare any two photos side by side, defaulting to your oldest and newest front-on shots; tap either to swap in a different one.
Training Type Breakdown — how your sessions split across Push/Pull/Legs/Whole Body.
Weekly Training Frequency — bars colour-coded by session type.
New SB / PB per Month — how fresh your records are.
Clean Days per Month — how many 🥗 No Cheat Meal days you logged.
Deload Timeline — when you took deload weeks.

Swipe any bar or line chart left or right to move through earlier periods; a ↩ Today button appears when you're away from the current one. Faint dashed lines running off the line chart edges mean there's data in the neighbouring period, and show the trend leading in or out of the view.
🖼️ Dashboard
Your home screen: last completed session and the next planned one (tap it to jump straight to that training tab), each showing how many days ago or away it is. Below that: weekly consistency streaks, weeks since your last deload, total training days with a yearly consistency ring, and a weekly frequency chart.

A 🛡️ Badges progress bar (tap it to jump to your Profile) and a 🥗 Clean Diet Days pair — your current streak and lifetime total of 🥗 days — sit below that.

Three leaderboards round it off — exercises with the longest-standing Increase flag, and those that haven't seen a new SB or PB in the longest time. Each row shows the training day, which position in the session the exercise sits at, its type, the weight and how long it's been — tap a row to jump straight to that exercise.
🥗 Diet
Your 🥗 Clean Diet Days streak and total sit at the very top. Enter your age, weight, height, body fat % and training frequency below for a daily calorie and macro target (Katch-McArdle formula), plus separate targets for a lean bulk and a cut — weight and body fat auto-fill from your latest Body Stats entry the first time, and a banner offers to update your targets if your Body Stats have since drifted from what you last calculated with.

Below that: a Clean Days per month chart, and a 💊 Supplementation Guide with dosing and a plain-language explanation for eight commonly used supplements — general reference info, not medical advice.
⚙️ Settings
📜 Record Log lists every SB and PB you've ever set, newest first. Swipe a row right to correct its weight or date, or left to delete it — the exercise's current record recalculates automatically from what's left.

Export your data (all of it, or just exercises, calendar, or body stats) as a JSON file you can back up or move to another device. Import restores from a backup. You can also clear specific data — like just your SB records — without wiping everything.
🔔 Notifications & Offline
Once added to your Home Screen, the app works offline and keeps the screen awake during a session. Rest timer alerts can arrive as push notifications even when the app isn't open, powered by a small free relay service. A separate reminder nudges you to measure again 24 days after a full Body Stats entry — delivered the next time you open the app on or after that day, since true background scheduling this far out isn't reliable on the free infrastructure this app runs on.
🎖️ Profile & Achievements
Tap the round avatar in the header to open your Profile. Set a nickname (type it, then tap the ✓ that appears, or hit Enter to confirm) and pick an avatar — a coloured preset, or your own photo from the camera or library. An Accent Color row lets you swap the app's orange highlight for one of seven other colours; the training-type, chart and badge-category colours stay fixed so nothing becomes ambiguous.

Below that, every badge you can earn is laid out by category — Training Consistency, Planning, Strength Milestones, Perfect Rests, Discipline, Body Tracking, Personalization and Clean Eating. Locked badges show a live progress count (like "62/100") so the goal is always visible; unlocked ones glow in their category colour. Badges pop up the moment you earn them, wherever you are in the app, and tapping any badge shows its full description and unlock date.
🙌 About This App
This app was designed and built by Marcin Zająkała from Poland, working together with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant — describing what to build and refining it turn by turn, with Claude writing all the actual code.

📱 Every single part of it was created on a phone. No laptop, no desktop, no code editor — the entire app was specified, written, tested and published from an iPhone. Want to get in touch? Reach out on LinkedIn.
It's open source — a single self-contained HTML file with no paid backend, hosted for free. It was built entirely with free tools:
  • GitHub Pages — free static hosting for the app itself
  • Documents by Readdle — used on the iPhone to preview and test each change before publishing
  • ntfy.sh — a free, open-source push notification service
  • Render.com — free-tier server that schedules and relays rest-timer notifications
  • Standard, built-in web technology — HTML, CSS and JavaScript, Service Workers for offline use, and SVG for the charts — no paid frameworks or libraries
Through the build and every round of refinement since — new features, layout fixes, bug hunts and sections like this one — a paid Claude subscription was used, powered by Anthropic's Sonnet and Opus models.
